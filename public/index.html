About SmartExpense AI

Objective & Purpose

SmartExpense AI was developed to help users manage their personal finances more easily and intelligently. It uses an AI to provide advice on spending, budgeting, and maintaining financial balance.

Perfect for students, employees, and individuals who want to plan their finances more wisely.

Key Features

Smart AI Advisor

Gives automatic advice based on your spending patterns and financial behavior.

Interactive Charts

Visualize your expenses using pie charts and detailed financial reports.

Savings Tracker

Track your savings progress and compare it against your financial goals.

Cloud-Based Access

Your data is securely stored online — not locally — so you can access your account anytime, anywhere.

User-Friendly Interface

Modern and responsive design suitable for all types of devices.

AI Spending Forecast

Predicts your future expenses based on past behavior and suggests ways to stay on track.

How It Works

1. Create Account

Register and set up your personal budget goals.

2. Track Spending

Input your expenses daily.

3. Get AI Advice

Receive intelligent tips to improve your savings.
